Welcome to my latest blog post! Here, I share exciting information about math animations in HTML.

In this post, I’ll cover various aspects of the topic, including convex hulls, lissajous figures and minkowski sums.

Convex hulls

Convex hulls of sets are the smallest sets that are convex and include the original sets. Here points of same color are a set and there convex hull is shown through the line. Convex hull

Lissajous figures

Lissajous figures illustrate the superposition of two perpendicular oscillations in x and y directions of different angular frequency (a and b). The relation of a and b determines the way the figure looks. Lissajous figures

Minkowski sums

Minkowski sums are created be the addition of two sets in such a way that the elements are added piecewise. Minkowski sum 1 Minkowski sum 2
